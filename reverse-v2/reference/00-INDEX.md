## The most important from this pass
- **[V] Motor PWM = TIMER2 CH3 (0x40000440)** — removes the advanced-timer hypothesis.
- **[V] The IMU is on I2C1 (PB10/PB11)**; but the physical chip (ICM-42688@0x68 vs BNO055@0x28) — [U], both are compiled in.
- **[V] chip2: there is NO digital coil gain** — the normalization = auto-baseline at startup (avg5 → RAM 0x20000094, deflt 2048) + thresholds (±2500/±5000). For the new coils, turn the knob here.
- **[V] ⚠️ chip2 has a hardware FLASH-CRC32 self-test (IEC60730, FUN_0801a5f0/FUN_080138a4)** — on failure the board hangs in a halt loop. Unlike chip1, our own firmware on chip2 without a CRC recomputation WILL BRICK the board.
- **[V] The RNG of the bounce angle = 75..135°** (FUN_0804f2c8(0x4b,0x87)); angle→ticks = angle*697/speed.
- **[U] remaining:** the real A4963 commutation (the cluster 0x0805f6xx without strings), the physical IMU chip, the RX callback of the chip2 link.
